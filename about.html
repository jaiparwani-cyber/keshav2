About

A short, slightly true history of our origins.

How it all began

Legend says the IISc Smart Mess started inside an abandoned chocolate factory. The story goes that a curious grad student discovered a hidden chute full of perfectly portioned samosas and, inspired by automated confectionery conveyors, decided the campus could use similarly efficient meal distribution. A prototype made from chocolate-wrapping machines, a 3D-printed ladle, and a very determined janitor later, the first "smart mess" was born.

While the chocolate conveyors were eventually retired (they attracted too many squirrels), the spirit lived on: streamline service, reduce waste, and make meals delightful. We kept the whimsical name and added real tech — menus, QR payments, and feedback loops — so students spend less time waiting and more time learning (or napping).

Mission

Deliver nutritious, affordable, and convenient meals with minimal waste and a dash of fun.
